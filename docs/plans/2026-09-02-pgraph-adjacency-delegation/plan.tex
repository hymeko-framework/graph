\section*{Compatibility inventory (\S1.1)}
\texttt{PGraphSchema}/\texttt{LoweredPGraph} are \textbf{not serialized} (no serde) --- internal changes have
\textbf{no wire impact}; \texttt{.pgip} is a SQLite emission over \texttt{inputs/outputs}, byte-checked by
the round-trip test. \texttt{schema.incoming/outgoing} is the \emph{single} canonical derived adjacency (the
one legit source). The \textbf{duplication} is the axiom-layer rebuilds: \texttt{axioms::build\_forward\_adj}
(== \texttt{schema.successors}), \texttt{axioms::build\_producers} (== \texttt{schema.predecessors} for
materials, by the bipartite invariant), and the \textbf{verbatim} \texttt{axiom\_extensions::\{build\_forward\_adj,
reaches\_any\}}.
\section*{Consolidation (\S1.3, atomic)}
One \texttt{reaches\_any(schema, from, targets)} (BFS over \texttt{schema.successors}), pub(crate), shared by
axioms + axiom\_extensions. Delete \texttt{build\_forward\_adj} ($\times$2), \texttt{build\_producers}, and
\texttt{axiom\_extensions::reaches\_any}. \texttt{check\_a2} uses \texttt{!schema.predecessors(m).is\_empty()};
\texttt{check\_a4}/\texttt{check\_strict\_no\_excess} use the shared \texttt{reaches\_any(schema,...)}.
\section*{Schema-adjacency removal --- DEFERRED (honest)}
Removing \texttt{schema.incoming/outgoing} entirely requires \texttt{LoweredPGraph} to \emph{borrow} the
\texttt{Ir}+\texttt{IncidenceIndex} (it is Ir-independent by design), lifetime-parameterizing the whole
domain layer (MSG/SSG/ABB/pgip/dumps). That cannot be done atomically with a byte-identical guarantee this
phase without large risk --- \textbf{deferred} to a scoped follow-up. \texttt{PGraphView} remains the
borrowed Ir-based adjacency/reachability, \textbf{proven equal} to the schema path (parity tests), used by
the AND/OR path.
